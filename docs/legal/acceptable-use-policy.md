# Acceptable Use Policy — DRAFT

> **⚠️ DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Engineering-authored starting point for
> counsel. Applies in full once user accounts and user-generated content go live;
> the general-conduct rules apply to all use today. _Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]._

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") supplements the
[Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) and governs how you may use Journee.

## 1. General conduct (applies to everyone, now)

You must not:

- break any applicable law or regulation, or facilitate others doing so;
- access, probe, or scan the Service or its systems without authorisation, or
  attempt to bypass security, rate limits, or access controls;
- scrape, harvest, or bulk-extract content or data except as expressly permitted;
- overload, disrupt, or degrade the Service (including denial-of-service);
- introduce malware or use the Service to distribute it;
- misrepresent your identity or affiliation, or infringe others' rights.

## 2. User-generated content rules (when UGC is live)

When you post notes, blogs, vlogs, reviews, ratings, or other content, you must
not submit content that:

- is unlawful, defamatory, fraudulent, or deceptive;
- is hateful, harassing, threatening, or incites violence or discrimination;
- is sexual content involving minors, or any content that sexualises minors
  (zero tolerance; reported to authorities as required by law);
- depicts or facilitates serious harm, dangerous activities, or illegal goods/
  services;
- infringes intellectual-property or privacy rights, or discloses others'
  personal data without consent;
- is spam, manipulated engagement, fake reviews, or undisclosed paid promotion;
- impersonates others or misrepresents travel safety in a way that could endanger
  people.

## 3. Reviews and ratings integrity

Reviews and ratings must reflect genuine experience. Incentivised, fake, or
coordinated rating manipulation is prohibited. Our rating system is designed to
resist manipulation (confidence-weighted scoring), but abuse may still result in
removal and account action.

## 4. Affiliate and commercial activity

You may not use the Service to run unauthorised commercial schemes, inject your
own affiliate codes, or misrepresent commercial relationships.

## 5. Enforcement

We may remove content, restrict features, or suspend/terminate access for
violations, in line with the
[Content Moderation & Takedown policy](./content-moderation-and-takedown.md). We
aim to act proportionately and to provide notice and a route to appeal where
appropriate and legally required (see DSA section there).

## 6. Reporting

Report violations via `[ABUSE/REPORT CONTACT]` or the in-product reporting tools
(when available).
