# Content Moderation & Takedown — DRAFT

> **⚠️ DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Engineering-authored starting point for
> counsel. Required **before any user-generated content goes live**, particularly
> for the EU (Digital Services Act) and US (DMCA). _Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]._

This document describes how Journee handles user-generated content ("UGC")
reports, moderation, and takedowns. It works alongside the
[Acceptable Use Policy](./acceptable-use-policy.md).

> **Status:** UGC features (posts/blogs/vlogs/reviews/ratings) are **not yet
> publicly available**. The pure logic cores exist, but storage, accounts, and
> moderation tooling are not deployed. This policy and the supporting tooling
> must be in place **before** UGC is enabled.

## 1. Notice-and-action (report a problem)

Anyone can report content they believe violates our rules or the law via
`[ABUSE/REPORT CONTACT]` or in-product reporting (when available). A valid report
should identify the content, the reason, and (for legal claims) enough detail to
assess it. We will acknowledge and act on valid reports within a reasonable time.

## 2. How we moderate

- Content may be reviewed proactively and in response to reports.
- We may remove or restrict content, label it, or limit its distribution.
- We may restrict or suspend accounts for repeated or serious violations.
- Decisions aim to be proportionate; automated tools may assist, with human review
  for significant actions where feasible.

## 3. EU Digital Services Act (DSA) commitments

When operating in the EU we will, as applicable to our size/role:

- provide an easy-to-use **notice-and-action** mechanism;
- give affected users a **statement of reasons** for moderation decisions;
- offer an **internal complaint/appeal** process and signpost out-of-court dispute
  settlement;
- maintain a **point of contact** for users and authorities;
- not use dark patterns; keep terms clear; and report transparency information
  `[scope depends on classification — confirm with counsel]`.

## 4. Copyright (DMCA / EU) takedowns

If you believe content infringes your copyright, send a notice to
`[DESIGNATED AGENT / COPYRIGHT CONTACT]` including: identification of the work and
the infringing material, your contact details, a good-faith statement, a statement
of accuracy, and your signature. We will remove or disable access to material that
is the subject of a valid notice and, where applicable, follow counter-notice
procedures. Repeat infringers will lose access.

## 5. Illegal content and emergencies

Content involving imminent harm or child sexual abuse material is removed
immediately and reported to the appropriate authorities/hotlines as required by
law. We preserve evidence where legally required.

## 6. Transparency and records

We keep records of reports and actions as needed for legal compliance and to
operate appeals, consistent with the [Data Retention policy](./data-retention.md)
and [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).
