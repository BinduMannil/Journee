## Our commitment

`[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` is committed to making Journee usable by as many people as
possible, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the **Web
Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) `[2.1 / 2.2]` level `[AA]`**, which also
underpins obligations such as the EU **European Accessibility Act (EAA)** and US
**ADA** expectations for consumer digital services.

## Current status

> **Honest status:** Journee's user interface is still being built; a formal
> accessibility audit has **not yet** been completed. This statement will be
> finalised with a real conformance assessment, known limitations, and audit date
> before public launch. Do not represent a conformance level that has not been
> tested.

Foundations already in place that support accessibility include: semantic HTML and
a design system, self-hosted fonts, and security headers. Areas requiring audit
include keyboard navigation, focus management, colour contrast, screen-reader
labelling, motion/animation preferences, and form/error handling.
